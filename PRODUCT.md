# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router, TypeScript), plain CSS. Confirmed by the user.

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers and freelance clients, weighted equally. They skim for a few seconds to judge whether Subash is worth a conversation, usually on a laptop.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Subash Paul, a software engineer. Success is a visitor downloading the resume PDF.

## Positioning

Full-stack engineer with delivery experience (low-code workflow product, MERN app, Azure DevOps CI/CD) plus ISRO computer-vision research and a 9.70 CGPA gold-medal degree.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Single-page site. Primary action: download resume (public/Subash_Paul_Resume.pdf). Public contact: email, LinkedIn, GitHub only. The phone number stays off the site.

## Evidence on Hand

Resume PDF only. Certificate, badge and live-demo links come from the resume's own hyperlinks (see lib/data.ts). No testimonials, metrics or screenshots exist; none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. The resume download is the single primary action and is always reachable.
2. Claim only what the resume states.
3. Skimmable in under ten seconds.
4. Quiet interface; the work and the name lead.
